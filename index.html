WHY IT MATTERS

A river is more than water.

The Ganga supports communities, ecosystems, agriculture, and cultural life. Protecting it requires awareness, responsible habits, and cooperation.

♻

Reduce waste

Choose reusable items and dispose of waste responsibly. Never leave litter near drains or riverbanks.

💧

Save water

Use water thoughtfully at home and encourage practices that reduce unnecessary water use.

🌱

Spread awareness

Share reliable information and support community-led river conservation activities.
